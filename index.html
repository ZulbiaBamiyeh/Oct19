Fib

20 yes/no questions to find today’s secret. One answer is a lie.

Start broad: “Is it alive?”, “Is it bigger than a car?”, “Can you eat it?”

    Basic matching

    How to play

    Everyone gets the same secret each day: a thing, a place, an animal or a person.

    • Ask up to 20 yes/no questions in your own words.
    • Answers are Yes No Sort of or Doesn't apply.
    • One of the first 8 yes/no answers is a lie. Ask questions that check each other.
    • Type your answer and press Guess when you know it. Wrong guesses cost a question.
    • When you finish, name the lie for bonus bragging rights.

    Asking the same thing twice is free, but you’ll get the same answer, lie included.
